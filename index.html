What's new in 0.2.0

Transport rebuilt for live WoW: Forever, where addon messages on custom channels are refused: your own guild lines are posted into the hidden channel as text from your keypress and reach every guild on every realm at once; lines from members without the addon are carried by your guild's courier by whisper. Realms no longer matter. Settings are now per character: Guild Info restores the channel and tag, nicknames must be set again. 0.1.x clients do not interoperate.

Install

  1. Unzip. You get a folder named CORELink.
  2. Move it into World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/, replacing any older CORELink folder.
  3. Log in, or type /reload if you were already in game. Type /cl to confirm the version.

If your guild's Guild Information already has a CORELink: line, you are done. Guild masters: /cl setup <channel> <password> [tag] and paste the line it shows into Guild Information.
